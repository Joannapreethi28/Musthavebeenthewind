# Production protocol — e-only-hour-end-v1

This document supersedes conflicting preliminary rules and probe thresholds in PLAN.md, DATA_SPEC.md, DESIGN_BRIEF.md, and VIVA_NOTES.md. It implements the research decisions in IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md before training or test evaluation.

- Use positive, quality-zero E2E_COR_FLUX only; do not average in or substitute W. Rodriguez et al. (2025), section 2 and Appendix A, identify GOES-15 W-sensor degradation: https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/67315/noaa_67315_DS1.pdf . Retain the agreed operational product and disclose its calibration/contamination limitations.
- Preserve actual five-minute timestamps. The March 2012 archive omits records. Validate unique ordered timestamps within the requested month, count absent grid positions, and leave them missing. Never reshape sparse records into consecutive hours. The final hourly grid still contains 52,608 rows.
- Average at least six valid samples in linear flux per UTC hour, floor valid means at 1, then take log10. Missing means remain missing.
- OMNI columns 17, 25, 41 are Bz GSM, speed and Dst; remove fill values. NASA confirms hour-start labels: https://omniweb.gsfc.nasa.gov/html/omni2_doc_addition.html . Use hour end for availability; apply no additional propagation shift. Archived processing latency is unmodelled.
- Issue time is row start plus one hour. Target is the row 12 hours later, ending at issue plus 12 hours. Issue and target end must lie within the same half-open period: train 2012–2015, validation 2016, test 2017. Earlier known history may supply a later period's first window. Never shuffle.
- Fill complete, bounded gaps of at most three hours, separately by availability period. Record the right support index. Accept histories only when both interpolation supports are known at issue time. Current anchors and future targets must be originally observed.
- Normalize four continuous features using finite unique training-available hours. Leave sine/cosine unchanged. Fit all diurnal bins on accepted training samples only. Models share identical validation/test samples.
- Old E/W coverage, daily amplitude and test PE are historical diagnostics, not production thresholds. Reproduce historical validation baselines separately. Record E-only coverage and exclusions before training. Use the measured corrected baseline in the report.
- Run the default LSTM with seeds 0/1/2, up to 60 epochs, patience 8, and the specified optimizer/loss. No tuning grid; omit the optional Transformer to meet the user's speed request, decided before test evaluation. Show seed 0 lines and mean scores with sample standard deviation across seeds.
- Freeze configuration and checkpoint hashes before final test evaluation. Report negative results honestly; do not tune from test scores.

Measured artifacts are results/data_quality.json, results/baselines.json, results/frozen_experiment.json and results/metrics.json as their phases complete. Raw/processed data, environments and checkpoints stay local and ignored.

The 2015 Dst minimum remains -234 nT. The complete-window and causal-label rules retain 34,020 train, 8,648 validation and 8,262 test samples. The corrected validation diurnal baseline PE is 0.70652, RMSE 0.61685. The separate historical validation probe passes its tolerance. Test outcomes have not been scored at this gate. The lower complete test-window count is explained by the recorded sequential missing-input exclusions, not a relaxed quality threshold.
