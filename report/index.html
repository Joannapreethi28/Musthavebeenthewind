03 / Rhythm

A forecast crosses the daily cycle.

The training flux peaks near local-time bin 11. Its peak-to-trough range is 0.732 log₁₀ units.

04 / Method

Learn the change. Preserve the clock.

A forecast is the observed current log-flux plus a predicted change. Time boundaries and missing observations are part of the model contract.

01 / ObserveGOES + OMNI

E-detector flux, wind speed, Bz, Dst and local-time sine/cosine.

02 / Window72 hours × 6

Training-only normalization; causal short-gap interpolation.

03 / Learn51,777 weights

Two LSTM layers; best validation checkpoint per seed.

04 / Forecast+12 hours

Current observed log-flux + predicted Δ. Observed targets only.

Limitations

This is retrospective forecasting with archived inputs; processing latency is not modelled. One satellite and one test year limit generalization. Adjacent windows overlap, so samples are not independent. The operational electron product may retain proton contamination and calibration error. Missing inputs exclude some hours, including storm intervals. The flux floor changes low-level observations. Seed spread measures training variability, not confidence over future storms. This experiment does not establish operational warning skill.

The optional Transformer and tuning grid were omitted before test evaluation to keep this course experiment bounded.
