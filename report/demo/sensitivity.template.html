What did it actually learn?

The explorer answers one hour at a time. This page sweeps a single input across its range at many hours at once and plots how the 12-hour forecast responds. It is the closest thing to asking the model what it believes about the solar wind.

How to read it. The curve is the median change in the 12-hour forecast, in log₁₀ flux units, when that input is shifted by the amount on the horizontal axis across the whole 72-hour history. The band is the interquartile range across sampled hours. A wide band means the model's response depends heavily on the situation, which is itself a finding.

What it is not. This is the model's response surface, not the magnetosphere's. A flat curve means this network barely uses that channel; it does not mean the channel is physically unimportant. Hours are sampled evenly across 2017, not chosen.
